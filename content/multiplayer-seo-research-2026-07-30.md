## Published route strategy

- English: six separate pages because the researched intents differ materially.
- German: separate Minecraft-with-friends and mobile-multiplayer-with-friends pages.
- Spanish: separate Minecraft-with-friends, phone-games-with-friends, and voice-chat pages.
- French and Italian: separate friends-game and Minecraft-with-friends pages.
- Japanese: separate phone-games-with-friends, Minecraft-with-friends, and voice-chat pages.
- Korean: separate mobile-online-games-with-friends and voice-chat pages.
- Portuguese, Arabic, Russian, Simplified Chinese, and Traditional Chinese: one native intent hub per market until reliable volume or Search Console impressions support another URL.
- Voice and push-to-talk phrases appear inside relevant pages. They do not create thin doorway URLs where measured demand is zero or very small.
- Every page clearly states the real control: Tiny Block uses a microphone on/off toggle. The phrase “push to talk” is retained only in an explanatory comparison that answers the search intent without claiming a hold-to-talk interaction.
