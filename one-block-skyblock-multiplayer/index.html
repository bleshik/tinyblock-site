One Block Skyblock Multiplayer

One Block is more fun when another player can help protect the tiny start, collect resources, and turn it into a living world.

Play Tiny Block online

Quick answer

Two ways to play One Block with friends

In Minecraft, multiplayer One Block usually starts with a compatible community map and a shared local world, server, or Realm. Tiny Block includes its own 2D One Block mode: install the free game, create a public or invite-code world, and grow it together without finding a separate map or hosting a Minecraft server.

Play together

How to start a multiplayer world

1

Join a public world

Open Multiplayer, browse the public world list, and join a world that is accepting players.

2

Create a private world

Choose an invite-code world when you want to control who can enter.

3

Share the invite code

Send the code to a friend. They enter it in Multiplayer to join the same persistent world.

4

Build and talk together

Split mining, crafting, building, and exploration while proximity voice follows your in-world distance.

Why it works

Choose the multiplayer experience that fits

Protect the tiny start

With two players, one person can mine while the other makes safe space, moves liquids, plants trees, or keeps creatures away from the edge.

No separate map search

Tiny Block's One Block world is a built-in mode rather than a third-party Minecraft map. Its progression is designed around Tiny Block discoveries.

A world that keeps changing

New structures and biomes are generated from discoveries, so the shared island develops beyond the first regenerating block.

Questions

Multiplayer FAQ

Can you play One Block Skyblock multiplayer?

Yes. Minecraft players can use a multiplayer-compatible One Block map and shared world. Tiny Block includes a free built-in One Block mode with public and invite-code multiplayer.

Do I need a server for One Block multiplayer?

That depends on the Minecraft edition and map. Local hosting, a server, or a Realm may be used. Tiny Block does not require you to configure a Minecraft server.

Is Tiny Block One Block free?

Tiny Block is free to download and play on iOS and Android.

Can friends talk inside a Tiny Block world?

Yes. Tiny Block multiplayer includes proximity voice with a microphone on/off toggle.
