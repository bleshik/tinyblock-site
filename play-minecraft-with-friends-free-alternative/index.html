Minecraft friends guide

Play Minecraft With Friends: Free Alternative

Minecraft offers several ways to play together. If cost or setup gets in the way, Tiny Block is a free independent mobile alternative built around shared worlds.

Quick answer

How to play Minecraft with friends

Use the same compatible Minecraft edition, sign in with the required account, enable multiplayer permissions, and then join a local world, featured server, or invited Realm. Console play may require a platform online subscription, and a Realm is an optional recurring subscription. If you want a free game you can enter together on iOS or Android, Tiny Block offers public and invite-code worlds without pretending to be Minecraft.

Questions

Multiplayer FAQ

Do you have to pay to play Minecraft with friends?

Minecraft itself is paid. Some multiplayer routes are included, while Realms and console online services can add subscriptions depending on platform.

Can I play a free game like Minecraft with friends on mobile?

Tiny Block is a free independent 2D survival crafting game for iOS and Android with public and invite-code multiplayer worlds.

Do all friends need the same Minecraft edition?

Minecraft multiplayer requires compatible editions and versions. Official Minecraft support documents the current requirements.

Is Tiny Block an official Minecraft game?

No. Tiny Block is created by No Such Games and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
