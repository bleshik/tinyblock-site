Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 21, 2026
Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how No Such Games (“we”, “us”) collects, uses, and shares information when you use Tiny Block on the web, iOS, and Android (the “Game”), and when you visit nosuchgames.com or tinyblock.nosuchgames.com (the “Websites”).

1. Who we are

Tiny Block is published by No Such Games. Privacy contact: privacy@nosuchgames.com.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Information stored on your device

Game progress, worlds, inventory, settings, and a random local player profile are stored on your device. This data stays local unless you choose to sync or share it through a feature we clearly describe later.

2.2 Anonymous installation / session data

To power discovery and procedural content features, the Game may create an anonymous installation identifier and session token, and send limited technical information such as platform name. We do not require an account, email, or real name to play.

2.3 Website analytics

The Websites use Google Analytics to understand visits and interactions. Google Analytics may process page URLs, referring pages, browser and device information, approximate location derived from IP address, and events such as store-link clicks or collaboration-contact clicks. In the EEA, United Kingdom, and Switzerland, analytics storage is denied by default.

2.4 Advertising data

The Websites and browser version of the Game may show ads through Google AdSense. On iOS and Android, the Game may show ads through Google AdMob. Advertising partners may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect device or browser identifiers, approximate location derived from IP address, and interaction data needed to serve, limit, and measure ads. Where required by law, we ask for consent before personalized advertising.

2.5 Technical and diagnostics data

We may collect crash logs, performance metrics, app version, device type, OS version, language, and similar technical information to keep the Game stable and fix bugs.

2.6 Information you send us

If you email us, we receive the content of your message and your email address so we can respond.

3. How we use information

  • Operate, maintain, and improve the Game
  • Provide procedural content and discovery features
  • Show ads and measure ad performance on the web and mobile
  • Understand Website traffic and measure store and collaboration interest
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, and technical issues
  • Respond to support requests and legal obligations

4. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers that host our backend or help us operate the Game
  • Google Analytics for Website measurement and Google / AdSense / AdMob for web and mobile advertising
  • Platform operators such as Apple or Google when required to distribute or run the Game
  • Authorities when required by law or to protect rights and safety

Google’s use of information is described in Google’s Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

5. Children’s privacy

Tiny Block is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Your choices

  • Reset or delete local game data from your device or by reinstalling the Game
  • Manage ad personalization through consent prompts where shown and in iOS/Android settings
  • Limit ad tracking / opt out of personalized ads using system controls
  • Use browser privacy controls to limit or delete Website cookies
  • Contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information we hold about you

7. Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete it. Server-side anonymous identifiers and related records are kept only as long as needed to operate discovery features, prevent abuse, and meet legal requirements, then deleted or aggregated.

8. International transfers

Our service providers and advertising partners may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses or your consent.

9. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised Policy.
