One Block starter guide

How to grow your floating island

Turn the first block into a safe, renewable base before exploring farther biomes.

01

Mine carefully

Keep enough space around your starting point and collect the first grass, dirt, stone, wood, water, and lava discoveries.

02

Create renewable stone

Combine water and lava for cobblestone, then use four cobblestone to craft stone.

03

Build a safe platform

Expand horizontally before digging deep. Keep fluids separated and leave room for trees and plants to grow.

04

Craft tools and storage

Turn wood into planks, then make a pickaxe and chest. Better tools shorten mining time and protect your discoveries.

05

Follow new biomes

Floating Islands introduce different terrain, plants, creatures, and recipes. Prepare for darkness, ice, lava, and aggressive night creatures.
