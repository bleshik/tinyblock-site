Minecraft × Tiny Block

What is similar — and what is different?

Minecraft crafting follows fixed 2×2 or 3×3 grid recipes. Tiny Block focuses on combining discoveries and on physical material reactions inside the world.

Recipe layout

Minecraft often cares where ingredients sit in a grid. Tiny Block recipes focus on the ingredient combination and discovered result.

World reactions

Water and lava, falling blocks, heat, and freezing can create or transform materials outside the recipe screen.

Discovery loop

Known results enter the catalog, while unusual combinations encourage experimentation and future discoveries.
