Minecraft × Tiny Block

What is similar — and what is different?

One Block is usually a community-made Minecraft map or server challenge. In Tiny Block, One Block is a built-in game mode with its own discovery loop.

Minecraft One BlockTiny Block
Starting worldA community-made Marketplace world starts the player on one regenerating block in 3D Minecraft.Tiny Block starts on one mineable block in a purpose-built 2D mode included with the game.
ProgressionBreaking the central block reveals resources chosen by the map creator; the player uses standard Minecraft crafting and building.The world grows through mining, material reactions, crafting, plants, and creatures unique to Tiny Block.
Fresh contentExtra islands, stages, bosses, and updates depend on the particular Marketplace map or server.Tiny Block generates a new structure daily and a new biome weekly, based on your discoveries.
