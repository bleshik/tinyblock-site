Voice-chat game guide

Multiplayer Games With Voice Chat

Voice can turn a multiplayer session from two silent avatars into an actual shared adventure. The right option depends on the world you want and the setup you accept.

Quick answer

A survival-building game with voice included

Tiny Block combines a free mobile survival sandbox with built-in proximity voice. Join a public world or share an invite code, toggle the microphone on when you want to talk, and explore: nearby players sound clear, and voices fade as distance grows.

Why it works

Choose the multiplayer experience that fits

Proximity changes the conversation

A friend's voice is strongest nearby and fades with distance. That makes exploring a cave or getting separated feel connected to the world.

Push to talk without holding a button

Tiny Block uses a push-to-talk-style microphone control without requiring a long press: tap once to turn the microphone on, then tap again to turn it off. Proximity volume still follows player distance.

Public or private sessions

Use a public world for an open multiplayer session or an invite code for a shared world with people you already know.

Questions

Multiplayer FAQ

Which mobile game has built-in proximity voice chat?

Tiny Block includes proximity voice with a microphone on/off toggle in its multiplayer worlds on iOS and Android.

Does Tiny Block use push to talk?

It uses a push-to-talk-style microphone toggle: tap once to start transmitting and tap again to stop, rather than holding the button.

Can I play and talk with friends without Discord?

Yes. Tiny Block voice is built into the multiplayer session, so a separate voice-call app is not required.

Does proximity voice work everywhere in the world?

Voice volume follows player distance. Nearby players are clear; distant players become quieter and eventually inaudible.

Can I make the multiplayer world private?

Yes. Create an invite-code world and share the code with the players you want to join.
