Survival, crafting, and co-op

Free Multiplayer Survival Game

Tiny Block is a multiplayer survival-building game about turning a tiny beginning into a world that keeps producing new materials, creatures, structures, and biomes.

Quick answer

Survival crafting that grows with both players

Create a public or invite-code world and decide how to divide the work. One player can mine while another builds, manages water and lava, grows plants, crafts tools, explores caves, or follows a new biome. Proximity voice keeps coordination inside the game.

Why it works

Choose the multiplayer experience that fits

Mine and build together

Split early survival tasks or work on the same structure. Shared materials and terrain make every action visible to the other player.

Explore day, night, and caves

Light, darkness, water, lava, habitats, and player actions change what the world reveals and how creatures behave.

More than a fixed recipe list

The documented recipes are starting discoveries. New combinations can expand the world with additional results and generated content.

Questions

Multiplayer FAQ

Is Tiny Block a free multiplayer survival game?

Yes. Tiny Block is free on iOS and Android and includes public and invite-code multiplayer worlds.

Can two players build the same world?

Yes. Multiplayer players share the world state, terrain, materials, creatures, and discoveries.

Is Tiny Block co-op or competitive?

The core multiplayer experience is cooperative sandbox survival and building rather than a round-based competitive match.

Does multiplayer include voice chat?

Yes. Tiny Block includes proximity voice with a microphone on/off toggle and distance-based volume.
