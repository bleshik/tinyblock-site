Quick answer

A free building game you can start together

Tiny Block is free to download on iOS and Android. Create a public world or an invite-code world, bring a friend into the same evolving 2D sandbox, and use built-in proximity voice while you mine, craft, build, and discover new biomes.

Why it works

Choose the multiplayer experience that fits

Build instead of compete

Tiny Block is designed around a shared world. Friends can expand terrain, experiment with materials, craft tools, meet creatures, and explore rather than enter a match that resets.

Choose public or invite-only

Browse a public world when you want to meet another player, or share an invite code when you want a private session with someone you know.

Keep talking inside the world

Toggle the microphone on when you want to talk. Proximity voice gets quieter as players move apart, so conversation follows the exploration instead of feeling like a separate call.

Questions

Multiplayer FAQ

What free phone game can I play with a friend?

Tiny Block is free on iOS and Android and supports public and invite-code multiplayer worlds.

Can iPhone and Android players play together?

Tiny Block multiplayer is available on both iOS and Android. Players join public worlds or use the same invite code.

Does Tiny Block have voice chat?

Yes. Multiplayer includes proximity voice with a microphone on/off toggle, so nearby players sound louder and distant players fade out.

Is Tiny Block Minecraft?

No. Tiny Block is an independent 2D survival crafting game by No Such Games and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.
