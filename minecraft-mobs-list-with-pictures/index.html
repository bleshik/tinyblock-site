Minecraft × Tiny Block

What is similar — and what is different?

Minecraft mobs use passive, neutral, and hostile behavior groups. Tiny Block creatures follow comparable roles, but light, water, caves, lava, plants, and player actions can change how they react.

Passive and fearful

Meadow Hopper, Forest Fox, Sky Mote, and Pool Drifter avoid danger instead of becoming resources to farm.

Defensive

Sand Crab and Moss Crawler fight back when disturbed, closer to a conditional neutral behavior.

Hostile by habitat

Dusk Prowler, Gloomwing, Cave Skitter, and Cinder Eel emerge under the right night, cave, or lava conditions.
