How it works

Try it first. Decide together.

  1. 1
    Play without obligation.

    We send access and context. If the game is not a fit, nothing is owed.

  2. 2
    Choose the story.

    We agree on the content idea, in-game reference, timing, and commercial terms.

  3. 3
    Approve the reference.

    You review the final name, description, and visual inspiration before it ships.

  4. 4
    Publish and measure.

    The relationship is disclosed clearly and tracked results are reported transparently.

Who this is for

Fit matters more than follower count.

We’re especially interested in creators covering mobile and indie discoveries, sandbox experiments, crafting and survival systems, cozy games, strange mechanics, and playful challenges. Small focused communities are welcome.

The clear version

No hidden catches.

  • Trying the game never requires a post or a positive opinion.
  • You approve how your public name or creator identity is referenced.
  • Paid and affiliate relationships must be disclosed to the audience.
  • Fees, tracking, rev share, usage rights, and duration are agreed in writing.
  • Creator references are intended as long-term parts of the game, not unconditional promises of permanence.
